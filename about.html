About

Ronald Katende

Mathematician working at the intersection of machine learning, scientific computing, numerical analysis, partial differential equations, and theoretical computer science.

Research identity

I am based in the Department of Mathematics at Kabale University, Uganda. My research focuses on the mathematical and computational foundations of learning under real-world constraints.

I am especially interested in problems where information is incomplete: few labels, sparse sensors, indirect observations, incomplete physical models, limited compute, unstable data distributions, or resource-constrained deployment environments.

Approach

The work is theory-first. I use proofs and structural analysis to isolate the mechanism, then use numerical experiments and software where they test something the mathematics alone cannot settle.
