Teaching & supervision

Mathematics in theory and practice.

I teach undergraduate and postgraduate mathematics, including numerical methods, differential equations, linear algebra, statistics, Python and R.

My supervision covers numerical analysis, mathematical modelling, scientific computing and machine learning. The aim is for students to formulate a precise question, understand their methods and produce results they can explain and reproduce.
