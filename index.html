Mathematics · Machine Learning · Scientific Computing

Learning and computation under real-world constraints.

I develop mathematical foundations, algorithms, and scientific computing methods for intelligent systems operating when data, sensors, computation, observations, or physical information are limited.

Research programme Current projects
How much can a system learn, recover, predict, or certify when information and computational resources are fundamentally constrained?
Research programme

Four connected directions

The work is theory-first, but each direction is motivated by systems that must operate without ideal data, unlimited computation, or perfect physical information.

01 · Foundations

Learning under information constraints

Label efficiency, observation limits, representation complexity, identifiability, stability, and information-theoretic limits on learning.

02 · Scientific ML

Inverse problems and structured learning

Physics-informed learning, partial differential equations, numerical analysis, sparse sensing, scientific digital twins, and residual certification.

03 · Complexity

Computation, architecture and representation

Depth-width tradeoffs, work-span structure, circuit complexity, nonlocal witnesses, and computational barriers to efficient learning.

04 · Decision readiness

Reliable AI under resource constraints

No-harm model selection, evidence efficiency, forecasting, uncertainty, deployment limits, and decision-support systems for constrained environments.

Research philosophy

Structure first. Evidence second. Deployment only when justified.

Prove the limit

Identify what information, representation, or computation is fundamentally necessary before optimising an algorithm.

Build the certificate

Expose assumptions, uncertainty, residuals, and failure modes instead of relying only on headline predictive performance.

Test the mechanism

Use numerical experiments and reproducible software when they answer questions the theory alone cannot settle.
