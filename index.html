The guiding question
What information and computation are sufficient for a reliable scientific result?

This question connects my work on PDE approximation, physics-informed learning, sparse sensing and decisions under uncertainty. It also sits within a longer-term interest in what it means for a machine to understand a scientific system.

Profile

Research shaped by mathematics and practice

My doctoral work at Makerere University, in collaboration with the University of Bergen, examined the numerical analysis of physics-informed neural networks for solving PDEs.

I now teach at Kabale University and work across mathematical analysis, reproducible numerical experiments and applications where limited data or resources affect reliability.
