Ongoing research · Under evaluation

Spectral Folding

Research problem. Fractional and nonlocal models carry history forward. Fixed compressed-memory representations can be efficient but may not match changing accuracy and cost requirements. Can one change the spectral representation online, without replaying history, and still certify the effect of the change on future computation?

Current work. Studying causal state-transfer rules, whole-bank replacement, compositional error accounting and low-overhead certificates. Existing experiments have also exposed the difficulty of outperforming appropriately matched fixed spectral banks.

What remains open. A general, rigorously justified adaptive policy with reproducible computational advantage over strong baselines across meaningfully different problems. No universal speed-up or superiority claim is made.
