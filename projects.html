Projects

Current lines of work.

Selected projects connecting mathematical questions to scientific computing, open research software, and constrained real-world systems.

Learning theory

Which Representations Need Fewer Labels?

Mathematical certificates for comparing representations by the amount of labelled data they require, linking geometry, information, stability, and sample complexity.

Architecture theory

Work–Span Architecture Frontiers

A theory-first programme connecting computation DAGs, work, span, parallel pressure, network depth and width, and representation complexity.

Complexity theory

Nonlocal Witnesses for Circuit Lower Bounds

A barrier-aware approach to Boolean circuit lower bounds that studies why local truth-table evidence is insufficient and what genuinely nonlocal certificates might require.

Scientific ML

Decision-Ready Digital Twins

Information limits, task-projected learning, sensor design, and engineering validation for digital twins operating under sparse sensing and limited resources.

Health AI

Evidence Efficiency in Health Artificial Intelligence

Auditing whether faster or cheaper model development translates into evidence strong enough for external validity, calibration, clinical utility, equity, and workflow deployment.

PDE theory

Navier–Stokes Gap Reduction

Analysis of ultraviolet completion, suitability, pressure closure, synchronization obstructions, and local-to-global mechanisms in three-dimensional incompressible flow.
