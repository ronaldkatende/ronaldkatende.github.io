\section*{Academic Profile}
Applied mathematician specializing in numerical analysis, scientific machine learning and reliable inference from incomplete observations. My research connects mathematical error and stability guarantees with sparse sensing, dynamical systems, data-efficient prediction and resource-constrained scientific decisions. I combine mathematical analysis, numerical experiments and applied modelling across engineering, climate, development and health.

\section*{Research Areas}
\cventry{Foundations}{Numerical analysis of PDEs; finite-element and neural approximation; stability and error certification; numerical linear algebra and low-rank computation.}
\cventry{Scientific ML}{Physics-informed neural networks; inverse problems; sparse-sensor recovery; operator learning; prospective risk control; long-memory dynamics.}
\cventry{Applications}{Industrial digital twins, resource-aware forecasting, climate and development analytics, epidemic and spatial models, infrastructure monitoring.}
