# Ronald Katende

**Mathematical foundations of reliable and efficient scientific computation**

I am a mathematician at Kabale University, Uganda. This repository hosts [my academic website](https://ronaldkatende.github.io/), bringing together my publications, research directions, selected projects, and academic background.

## Research

My research is guided by a central question:

> What information and computation are sufficient for a reliable scientific decision?

I work across three connected areas:

- **Reliable scientific computation:** mathematical analysis of stability, approximation, error, and certification in numerical methods and scientific machine learning.
- **Information sufficiency:** identifiability, inverse problems, sparse observations, and the information needed to support a particular scientific task.
- **Adaptive computation:** methods that reorganize computational representations as a problem evolves, particularly in systems with nonlocal or fractional memory.

### Spectral Folding

**Spectral Folding** is an ongoing research direction within adaptive computation. It investigates whether compressed spectral representations of long-memory dynamics can be reconfigured online, without replaying the full input history, while controlling the error introduced by those changes.

The emphasis is on mathematical guarantees, reproducible algorithms, and rigorous comparisons with established fixed-representation methods. Current work is identified as *research in progress*, rather than as a finished general-purpose solution.
