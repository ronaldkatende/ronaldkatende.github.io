Books

These books grew from questions encountered in mathematics, computation and practical problem-solving. They are written to be read carefully, tested, and used in teaching. The available editions remain open to correction and independent review.

From Problems to Working Solutions

Applied Mathematics, Computing and AI for Resource-Constrained Environments

How do we recognise a real problem, build a mathematical representation, decide what evidence is sufficient, and test whether a proposed solution actually works? This book follows that journey through modelling, data, optimisation, AI, software and evaluation.

Working manuscript · 7 chapters

Machine Memory

Mathematics, Algorithms, Architectures and Reliable Computation

What must a computation remember to answer later questions? The book studies exact and approximate state, streaming algorithms, dynamical systems, learning and replay, fractional memory, online state reconfiguration and the practical verification of memory systems.

About the book and access the manuscript

The books are working educational manuscripts. A reproducible demonstration is not, by itself, evidence of improved field performance. Some experiments rely on externally hosted datasets or archived measurements that require independent verification.
