From Problems to Working Solutions

Applied Mathematics, Computing and AI for Resource-Constrained Environments

Ronald Katende

A problem usually reaches us as a complaint: patients are waiting too long, a water supply is unreliable, or a clinic has run out of medicines. Before choosing an algorithm, we have to understand what is happening, what can be changed, and what evidence would make a decision defensible.

This book follows that journey. It begins with problems and measurements, develops the mathematics of models and decisions, then turns to prediction, optimisation, computing, implementation and evaluation. The examples often come from settings where money, data or infrastructure are limited, but the principles apply far beyond those settings.
