Printable Records for a Working Solution

This appendix provides forms that a student, lecturer or practitioner can adapt. They are not substitutes for local institutional policies or professional judgement. Their purpose is to keep decisions and evidence visible. The same forms can be maintained as plain text files or spreadsheets when printing is expensive.

Problem Definition Record

Question Response to complete before choosing a method
What is going wrong? Observable outcome, not just a proposed solution
Who is affected? Groups, locations and possible unequal consequences
Who can act? Named authority or decision maker
What can change? Set of feasible actions, including doing nothing
What matters? Outcomes, constraints, safety standards and equity
What is already known? Data, measurements, expert and local knowledge
What is missing? Most decision-relevant unknowns
What would count as success? Concrete measure and meaningful threshold
What could fail? Model, data, software, institution and unintended harms
What is the next step? Measurement, modelling, trial, deployment or withdrawal

Data Dictionary and Quality Record

For each field, include an unambiguous definition. A short worked pattern is:

Field Definition Unit / type Quality and permission
stock Physical units counted at agreed time count Count date, identifier, verifier
texttt{issue} Units dispensed in recorded interval count Censored by shortages; not demand
texttt{lead_time} Delay from approved order to receipt days Record definition and supplier

The data quality record includes source and retrieval date; permissions and licensing; intended population; missing-value mechanism; sensor or survey uncertainty; temporal and geographical resolution; units; cleaning actions; and what claims remain unsupported. Store the original data separately from cleaned files. Do not overwrite the source after transformations.

Deployment, Maintenance and Transfer Records

A handover record identifies the service owner, technical custodian, data steward, domain reviewer, ordinary support process, security permissions, recovery objectives, tested backup, annual budget and withdrawal procedure. A transfer record identifies the source and new setting, unchanged mathematical structure, changed measurements, target population, new constraints, external validation, adaptation costs and the final adoption decision. These documents can be short, but they should be owned and revised by named people rather than living only in a researcher’s notebook.

One rule for every form.

A blank unknown is preferable to an invented fact. Write unknown and state how it could be measured. Reliable innovation often begins when an institution can see exactly what it does not yet know.
